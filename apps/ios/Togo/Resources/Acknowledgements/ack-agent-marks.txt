Agent marks

Togo shows a small one-colour mark beside each coding agent it can start, so you can tell
them apart at a glance. The marks identify the agents only. Togo is not affiliated with,
sponsored by or endorsed by any of the companies or projects named below, and each name and
mark remains the trademark of its owner.

Every mark is the owner's own vector artwork. Togo only scales it to its symbol size and
draws it in its own text colours; its shape is unchanged. Where a mark has more than one
colour, its parts are drawn in one colour, and details drawn in the background colour are
left open.
